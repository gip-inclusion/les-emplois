{% comment %} Zendesk Talk widget (callback request), shown along the orientation tunnel to users coming from the DORA campaign (mtm_campaign=DORA, stored in session by the tunnel entry point). This is a temporary solution to facilitate the transition of DORA users who may struggle to understand how to create orientations in this application. The widget writes ZD-* identifiers to localStorage on load, so it requires consent: it is registered as a tarteaucitron service instead of being loaded directly. The service is only pushed on the pages that may show it, so it does not appear in the consent panel for the (vast majority of) users the widget never loads for.
